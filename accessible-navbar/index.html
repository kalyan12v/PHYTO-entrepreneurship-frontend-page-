About Phyto

Phyto is an innovative solution that uses artificial intelligence to accurately detect and diagnose plant diseases. Our goal is to empower farmers and researchers with precise, instant insights into crop health to minimize loss and maximize yield.

Our Vision

Phyto envisions a future where technology and agriculture work hand in hand to create a more resilient and productive farming ecosystem. By harnessing the potential of AI, we aim to empower farmers with the tools they need to enhance crop health, maximize yield, and ensure food security.
